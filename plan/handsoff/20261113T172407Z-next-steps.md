## Open decisions

1. Size of the overlay: a modal takes a room when one exists, so the
   menu comes up 48x13, or 34x9 on a busy screen, in place of a large
   one over the text. Either a floor (no room for 48x13 or more: over),
   or always over.
2. `stop --all` ends clients started elsewhere too (`ptyrc attach`, a
   Python console); in the menu, `x` and `p` on a client act without a
   confirmation.
3. `manage restart APP` without options keeps the old ones: no way to
   clear them from the shell.
4. A live move for apps that read their frame's text (snake, bricks,
   fishes, flower): they are restarted today.
