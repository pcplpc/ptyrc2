## What this session learned about piloting

- One app an agent, the family's writer first, each report's "for the
  next agent" pasted into the next brief: 24 apps, no rework. A fix
  agent on the driver beside an app agent: disjoint grounds held.
- The pilot's brief said "the launcher owns `--at`"; the guide's
  Decided list keeps it for four apps. An agent caught it. Point to
  the guide; do not paraphrase it.
- Three agents passed the cap (267k, 314k, 259k). "Stop at 220k" in
  the brief held better than "250k at most".
- An agent's commit took another's staged deletion once (`e119294`);
  the file came back. The check of `git diff --cached` before each
  commit is now in every brief.
- Timing under another agent's build is not a measure: seven apps
  were measured again at the end, one was over the bound.
